Easy Chocolate Pancakes

Super soft pancakes with sweet Chocolate flavor-ready in 15 minutes!

Preparation time : 15 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons cocoa powder
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon baking soda
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter or oil
  • ½ teaspoon vanilla extract
  • ¼ cup chocolate chips (optional)
  • Butter or oil for frying

Steps (Ready in 15 min!)

  1. In a bowl, whisk together flour, cocoa powder, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
  2. In another bowl, beat the egg, then add milk, melted butter, and vanilla → mix well.
  3. Pour wet ingredients into dry ingredients → stir until just combined (don't overmix).
  4. Fold in chocolate chips if using → let batter rest 2 minutes.
  5. Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat → add a little butter/oil.
  6. Pour ¼ cup batter per pancake → cook 1-2 minutes until bubbles form on top.
  7. Flip carefully → cook 1 more minute until cooked through.
  8. Serve warm with syrup, whipped cream, or fresh berries!
